Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

This website is operated by NextStopGuides (“we”, “us”). We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible. Contact: email us.

1. What data we collect

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address is stored by our email service provider only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
  • Purchases: guides are sold on Etsy and Shopier. Payments and order data are processed by those platforms under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details.
  • Language preference: your chosen language (EN/TR) is stored in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Analytics: we currently do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes, we will update this page and ask for consent where required.

2. Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling from the Tailwind CSS CDN, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. Links to Etsy, Shopier, Airalo, Booking.com and GetYourGuide take you to websites with their own privacy policies.

3. Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this website are affiliate links, including links to Airalo (eSIM), Booking.com (hotels) and GetYourGuide (tours). If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked with rel="sponsored".

We only recommend services we genuinely use or believe are useful for travelers. Commissions help us keep our free content and guides up to date. All brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners; NextStopGuides is not owned by or officially affiliated with these companies beyond their partner programs.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.
